How to play

  • Use the letter your opponent sent, or take a letter from the pool.
  • Build a word with the letters on your rack.
  • One letter from your word goes back to them.
  • Play your turn when you can. Several matches can stay open at once.
  • There are no ads. Optional coins buy looks, hints, extra match slots, and chat unlocks. WordAce Club is a monthly membership with extra coins and slots. Coins do not buy a different dictionary.

Quick help

  • Your-turn notifications — turn on game notifications in Profile (the label is localized) and allow notifications in iOS Settings → WordAce.
  • Stuck match / wrong score — email your username, opponent, and approximate time. A screenshot helps.
  • Coins / purchases — restore via Apple (Settings / Store). If the balance is wrong after a successful purchase, send the App Store receipt date.
  • Chat — unlocking chat costs coins once per person on your account; they unlock separately.
  • Delete account — Profile → Delete account (localized), or follow account deletion.

Quick help

  • Your turn notifications — enable “Sıra bildirimleri” in Profile and allow notifications in iOS Settings → WordAce.
  • Stuck match / wrong score — email us your username, match opponent, and approximate time. Include a screenshot if you can.
  • Coins / purchases — restore via Apple; if balance is wrong after a successful purchase, send the App Store receipt email date.
  • Chat unlock — unlocking chat costs coins once per peer for your account; the other player unlocks separately.
  • Delete account — use in-app “Hesabı sil” or follow account deletion.

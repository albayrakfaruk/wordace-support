Delete your account

Apple App Store requirement — permanent deletion path.

In the app (preferred)

  1. Open WordAce → Profile.
  2. Tap Hesabı sil / Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Deletion is queued; you can cancel from the same screen until it completes.

By email

Send from the email linked to your Sign in with Apple / account to support@veloratech.com with subject WordAce Account Deletion and your username.

What we delete

  • Profile, auth tokens, friends, chat threads you own, push tokens.
  • Active match participation is resigned/closed; historical match rows may be anonymized.

Timing

We complete deletion or anonymization within 30 days of a verified request. Some security/accounting logs may remain longer where legally required.
